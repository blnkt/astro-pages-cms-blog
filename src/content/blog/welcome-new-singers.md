We welcome singers of all experience levels. If you have been thinking about joining, come to our **first Spring rehearsal on Thursday, January 22** (6:30–8:15 pm, Taborspace Annex) and see how it feels before committing.

There is no audition. We do ask that you can match pitch reasonably well and plan to attend rehearsals and our **May 22** performance (up to three absences allowed). Questions about dues, scholarships, or vocal support? [Contact us](../../contact/).
